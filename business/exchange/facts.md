# [EXCHANGIFY] — DEMO CONTENT

This is a fictional business used to demonstrate the assistant. Every fee,
limit and time below is sample text, not a real offer. Do not present it as real.

## About

[EXCHANGIFY] is a demonstration crypto exchange front end. It exists to show how
the assistant answers questions from a fixed facts file. There is no real
trading, no real custody and no real money involved.

## Services & prices

Buy and sell BTC, ETH and USDT with NGN by bank transfer.

Demo fee table (sample values, not an offer):
- Buy: 1.5% of the NGN amount
- Sell: 1.2% of the NGN amount
- Network fee: shown in the app before you confirm

Demo KYC tiers (sample values):
- Tier 1: name and phone. Limit NGN 50,000 per day.
- Tier 2: government ID and selfie. Limit NGN 500,000 per day.
- Tier 3: proof of address. Limit NGN 2,000,000 per day.

## Hours

Support is available Monday to Friday, 9:00 am to 6:00 pm West Africa Time.
The app itself runs at all hours.

## Areas

The service is online-only. It is available across Nigeria. There is no
physical office and no walk-in service.

## How to order/book

1. Create an account in the app.
2. Complete the KYC tier you need for your amount.
3. Choose buy or sell, enter the amount, and confirm.

## Payments

- Deposits in NGN are by bank transfer to the account shown in the app.
- Crypto deposits are credited after the required network confirmations.
  BTC needs 2 confirmations, ETH needs 12, USDT on TRON needs 19.
- NGN withdrawals are processed on business days, Monday to Friday. Requests
  made after 4:00 pm or at the weekend are processed the next business day.
- Withdrawal processing is not instant and no exact minute can be promised.

## Policies

- Staff never ask for your password, OTP, PIN or seed phrase.
- Staff never message you first on any channel. Treat any first contact as fraud.
- Refunds for failed transactions go to the sending details on record.
- A compliance review can hold a transaction for up to 24 hours.

## Tone

Plain, calm and short. No hype. Never suggest that any asset will rise or fall
in value.

## Never do

- Never give investment advice or a recommendation to buy or sell.
- Never predict a price or a market direction.
- Never give tax or legal advice.
- Never quote a live rate. Live rates are shown in the app.
- Never confirm a withdrawal will arrive at a specific minute.
